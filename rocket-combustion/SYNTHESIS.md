# Synthesis — High-Frequency Rocket Combustion Instability (`000`–`011`)

This monograph synthesizes twelve verified open-notebook entries (`000`–`011`), audited across **seven independent cross-model peer reviews** (Claude on `000`–`008` and `010`; double-model Claude + Antigravity Gemini on `009`; Entry `011` pending Peer Review #8; receipts archived in [`../reviews/README.md`](../reviews/README.md)), building from a 1D Crocco $n\text{--}\tau$ baseline through 2D nonlinear acoustics and 1D/2D compressible Euler up to **2D $(x, \theta)$ conservative compressible Navier–Stokes with steady axial throughflow, no-slip viscous baffle wakes, and nonlinear wide-mouth faceplate cavity rings** (`009`–`011`).

Every law below is backed by an exact analytical derivation, continuum dispersion root, or Fourier-collocation linear eigen-spectrum **cross-checked directly against time-domain PDE integration**—with discrete energy-balance residuals ($10^{-8}\text{--}10^{-5}$) in bulk-linear `000`–`006`, machine-precision finite-volume conservation ($4\times 10^{-16}$) and per-mode numerical-damping audits in compressible Euler `007`–`008`, plus grid and long-horizon convergence checks.

## 2. Documented Dead Ends & Traps (What Broke)

1. **Unfiltered pressure-coupled Crocco law (Peer Review #1 C1):** Produces an unphysical static $k = 0$ bulk pressure runaway for every $n > \zeta$. Caught by real-axis scanning and fixed uniformly with $H_{\text{hp}}(s)$.
2. **Sign-reversing quintic flame laws (Peer Review #1 C2):** Polynomial subcritical flames $q + c_3 q^3 - c_5 q^5$ reverse sign at finite amplitude and drive $p'/\bar{p} \sim 0.55$, invalidating linear bulk acoustics. Replaced with strictly monotonic bounded $\Phi(q) = q_s\tanh(q/q_s + \kappa(q/q_s)^3)$ keeping $p'/\bar{p} \le 0.09$ in 1D and $\le 0.06$ on subcritical 2D branches.
3. **Under-damped cavities ($\xi_H \ll \xi_H^{\text{EP}}$):** Split the chamber mode into two weakly damped peaks that remain linearly unstable even at exact tuning (confirmed in 1D `002` and 2D `005`).
4. **Over-damped or tight-neck cavities ($\xi_H > \xi_H^{\text{EP}}$ or $\sigma_o = 1.5\%$):** Choke the cavity neck during finite-amplitude pressure pulses, collapsing bomb-survival thresholds despite strong linear damping (`003`, `006`).
5. **Short radial baffles ($L_b < x_f$):** Amplify rather than damp chiral spinning $1T$ (`004`).
6. **Conservative baffles under combined pressure + velocity driving:** Cannot dissipate pressure-coupled Rayleigh energy and can even worsen linear growth via phase detuning (`006` Exp 2: $+0.0166$ vs. bare $+0.0049$).
7. **$M = 2$ azimuthal lag staggering:** Bragg-resonates with $1T$, destabilizing the orthogonal standing orientation by $+67\%$ (`005`).
8. **Short-horizon bomb bisection without slow-mode time-scale matching (Peer Reviews #2 C1 & #3 C3):** Horizons of $t = 130\text{--}180$ were shorter than the $80/20$ cavity ring's least-damped linear time scale ($\tau = 1/|\alpha_{\min}| \approx 550$), hiding slow regrowth of large wide-mouth bombs ($A_{\text{eq}} \ge 0.045$) and non-monotonic behavior near the marginal manifold. Fixed in `006` with $t = 1000$ broadband linear verification and a $t = 900$ dense amplitude scan (no monotonicity assumption) with strictly non-decaying trigger criteria.
9. **Numerical-dissipation masking of weak high-harmonic instability (Peer Review #5 C1):** Using `minmod` at $N = 256$ introduces $-5.64\times 10^{-3}$ excess numerical damping on $3T$, hiding the $80/20$ ring's true linear $3T$ growth ($\alpha = +0.00191$ in `007`, $+0.00192$ in `008`) by $10{,}000\times$ at $t = 1600$. Fixed by pairing every compressible Euler cavity/stagger study with an exact Fourier-collocation linear eigen-spectrum, a per-mode numerical-damping audit, and the low-dissipation MC limiter.
10. **Unclosed zero-throughflow Euler rings ($\nu_{\text{res}} = 0$, Peer Review #5 C2 & M3):** Without axial propellant replenishment, quadratic nozzle enthalpy and momentum extraction drive secular mean-density ($\bar{\rho}$) and negative mean-swirl ($\bar{u}$) drifts, preventing stationary limit cycles and destroying the smooth chiral branch $S^*(n_v)$ via entropy/$c(\theta)$ scattering. Fixed explicitly in `007`–`008` via $\nu_{\text{res}}$ sensitivity tables and resolved from first principles in `009` via 2D physical axial throughflow.
11. **Nonlinear subcritical triggering of odd $M = 5$ lag staggering & the $2T$ Strouhal Harmonic Trap (`009`, Peer Review #6):** (a) At $\bar{M} = 0.12, n_p = 0.22, n_v = 0.04$, odd $M = 5$ staggering is strictly linearly stable ($\alpha = -0.0067$, decays under `linear_flame=True`), yet small linear margin plus $6.7\text{--}6.9\times$ Erlang-4 cubic gain asymmetry ($|H_1(i)| = 0.963$ vs. $|H_2(i)| = 0.506$) triggers a $3.4\text{--}3.7\%$ rotating limit cycle at $A_0 = 0.015$ on `48×144` (masked at `32×96`). (b) Tuning $\bar{M}$ to the half-integer $1T$ cold-damping peak ($\bar{M} = 0.16, \mathrm{St}_{1T} \approx 1.5$) drops $2T$ into an integer Strouhal trough ($\mathrm{St}_{2T} \approx 3.0, n_{pH,2T} = 0.2354$), generating a $6.53\%$ $2T$ rotating limit cycle on `48×144` that coarse `32×96` vorticity damping hid.
12. **Over-long baffles ($L_b = 0.60$ phase shift + hidden $2T$), detuned/blade-root hybrids, and coarse-grid orifice-cliff shift (`010`, Peer Review #7):** (a) Extending $N_b = 5$ baffles from $L_b = 0.45$ to $0.60$ at $\bar{M} = 0.12$ downshifts $\omega_{1T}$ by $-14.5\%$, re-destabilizing $1T$ by $2.48\times$ (`60×160`, worse than bare even at $n_v = 0$) and exciting an unstable $2T$ mode (`+0.0134` on `80×240`). (b) Tuning a $2\%$ hybrid cavity to unbaffled `0.989` wastes $79\%$ of margin (`-0.0045` vs. `-0.0214` at reacting `0.900`), and placing ports at blade roots (`phase = 0.0`) loses `-37%` margin (`-0.0135`). (c) On `48×160`, numerical $2T$ damping falsely makes $\sigma_o = 2.5\%$ bleed (`0.44%`), whereas `64×200` exposes a sustained $4.03\%$ $2T$ shock (`l/m = 1.000`; true orifice-drag cliff sits in `2.5–5.0%`).

## 3. Verified Scope vs. Open Problems

### What Is Verified Within This Model
- Linearized 1D and 2D thin-annulus bulk acoustics (`000`–`006`, zero mean Mach advection, no radial $r$-modes) with discrete energy-balance checks ($10^{-8}\text{--}10^{-5}$).
- 1D periodic compressible Euler transverse shock steepening, chiral swirl competition, even-$|u|$ speed coupling, cavity cascade absorption, and azimuthal lag staggering (`007`–`008`, $\gamma = 1.25$, MC + HLLC, inviscid conservation $4\times 10^{-16}$, Fay–Burgers $O(u_0^3)$ verification, exact Fourier-collocation eigen-spectra, and explicit $\nu_{\text{res}}$ closure sensitivity).
- 2D $(x, \theta)$ conservative compressible Euler annular combustor with physical steady axial throughflow, protruding solid-wall reflecting baffles, and nonlinear faceplate Helmholtz cavity rings (`009`–`010`, $\bar{M} \in [0.06, 0.30]$, choked zero-swirl injector, exact Marble–Candel choked nozzle, zero ad-hoc $S_{\text{ent}}$, steady-state preservation `1.1e-16`, periodic conservation `1.9e-16`, Chebyshev 2D linearized eigen-spectra, per-mode `80×240` damping audits, baffle–cavity reacting-frequency retuning & mid-sector placement, and `64×200` orifice-drag cliff).

### High-Value Open Problems for Future Entries (`011+`)
1. **Full 3D Cylindrical $(r, \theta, x)$ Chamber with Radial ($1R$) Modes:** Extend the 2D thin annulus to finite radial thickness, coupled $1R/1T$ modes, and hub-vs.-shroud radial baffle / acoustic-liner placement.
2. **Equal-Gain Pure-Delay Staggering & Stochastic Mode Switching:** Test whether equal-amplitude pure convective delays eliminate Entry `009`'s $6.7\text{--}6.9\times$ Erlang-4 subcritical asymmetry, and add stochastic turbulence forcing (Noiray & Schuermans).
3. **Viscous / Thermal Boundary-Layer & Nozzle-Contour Entropy-Vorticity Losses:** Couple Navier–Stokes viscous shear at baffle tips and finite-length convergent–divergent nozzle geometry beyond the compact Marble–Candel limit.
